TAD Chat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what TAD Chat ("the Service") collects and stores today, and the rights the operator, Lawrence Tong ("the operator"), reserves going forward. It should be read together with the Terms of Service, which is incorporated into this Policy by reference and governs your use of the Service, including its limitation of liability, indemnification, and binding arbitration provisions, all of which apply to any dispute about this Policy or the handling of your data.

Short version: TAD Chat stores the username you pick and the messages you post, in a shared database, so the chat works, plus a few things in your own browser. It also logs the IP address, some basic device/network telemetry (browser, screen size, time zone, language, platform), and some basic behavioral telemetry (compose time, pasting, tab switching, referrer, session message count) each message is sent with, none of which is ever shown to other users, but which may be used to help look into a suspected academic honesty violation. As described below, the operator does not currently sell your data, but reserves broad discretion over how data described in this Policy is used, retained, and disclosed, and may change this Policy at any time. Read the full Policy, not just this summary.

1. What is collected and where it's stored

DataWherePurpose
Username you choose Server (Upstash Redis) Identifies your posts to other users; not verified against any real identity.
If you create an account: your username, the school you select, and a hash of your password (the password itself is never stored) Server (Upstash Redis) Reserves your username so nobody else can post under it, and lets you sign in. The school is self-reported and not verified.
If you sign in: a sign-in cookie named session (a random token, valid for up to 30 days or until you log out). The server keeps only a hash of the token and the time it was created. Your browser (cookie) and server (Upstash Redis) Keeps you signed in. The cookie is not readable by page scripts and is not used for tracking or analytics.
Messages you post (text + timestamp + your username) Server (Upstash Redis) Displayed in the forum you posted to.
The IP address your device sends the message from Server (Upstash Redis), attached to the message record Not shown to other users or displayed anywhere in the app. Kept so the operator can help establish where a message came from — for example, if a suspected academic honesty violation needs to be looked into and location or device information would help confirm or rule out who was involved.
Device and network telemetry: browser/OS identifier (user agent), screen resolution, time zone, browser language, device platform, approximate device memory and CPU core count, and network connection type (e.g. wifi vs. cellular speed class) Server (Upstash Redis), attached to the message record Not shown to other users or displayed anywhere in the app. Kept for the same reason as the IP address above — to help distinguish or match up devices if a suspected academic honesty violation needs to be looked into.
Behavioral/interaction telemetry: how long you spent composing a message before sending it, whether the text was pasted in rather than typed, how many times you switched away from the tab while composing, the page that referred you here (if any), and how many messages you've sent so far this browser session Server (Upstash Redis), attached to the message record Not shown to other users or displayed anywhere in the app. Kept so the operator can distinguish a message that was actually typed out from one that was pasted in wholesale (for example, a copied answer), or spot other patterns (like stepping away to look something up mid-message) relevant to a suspected academic honesty violation.
Your chosen handle (a copy) Your browser (localStorage) So the site remembers your username between visits.
Which messages you've seen, per forum Your browser (localStorage) Powers the unread-message indicator.
A background image, if you set one Your browser (localStorage) Personalizes your own view; not sent to the server or seen by anyone else.

Aside from the IP address and device/network telemetry described above, and the sign-in cookie and account details described above if you choose to create an account, TAD Chat does not currently use cookies, run analytics or tracking scripts, or collect email or any identity information beyond the username (and, for accounts, the school) you enter. The operator reserves the right to add any of the above in a future version of the Service, in which case this Policy will be updated as described in Section 8.

Standard web server and hosting infrastructure logs (such as those generated automatically by Render, Vercel, and Upstash in the course of operating the Service) may exist outside the application's own database and are governed by those providers' own practices, not by this Policy.

2. No verified identity

Usernames are first-come, first-served and are not tied to your real name, school login, or email in any way the Service enforces. Anyone can type any username that is not already taken. Creating an account reserves a username so that nobody else can post under it, but the school you select is self-reported and is not verified. Do not assume a username belongs to who it claims to.

3. How long data is kept, and the operator's retention discretion

Messages and usernames are kept indefinitely by default — there is currently no automatic expiration or scheduled deletion. One reason for this: as described in the Terms of Service, TAD Chat exists for discussing how to approach schoolwork, not for exchanging completed answers, and keeping a durable record of what was actually said helps deter and, if needed, look into suspected academic dishonesty — including after the fact, and even if a user later asks for a message to be removed. The operator otherwise reserves the right to retain, archive, or delete any data described in this Policy, in whole or in part, at any time, for any reason or no reason, at the operator's sole discretion, including data covered by a request under Section 5. The operator makes no commitment as to how long any deletion request will take to process, or that any data will in fact be deleted rather than merely hidden from display, retained in backups, or retained where the operator determines retention is warranted (for example, for academic honesty review, moderation records, legal compliance, or dispute resolution).

4. Who can see it, and disclosure

Messages posted to a forum are visible to anyone using the Service. The underlying database is accessible to the Service's operator and to any person or service the operator engages to help operate, host, maintain, or improve the Service (for example, its hosting and database providers). The operator does not currently sell data collected through the Service to third parties, but reserves the right to disclose any data described in this Policy where the operator determines, in its sole discretion, that disclosure is appropriate — including to the school (for example, in connection with a suspected academic honesty violation, honor council proceeding, or teacher inquiry), to law enforcement, to comply with a legal obligation, to protect the rights, safety, or property of the operator or any person, to investigate suspected violations of the Terms of Service, or in connection with a transfer, sale, or restructuring of the Service or its underlying project.

5. Requesting removal of your data

You may request that your username or messages be removed by contacting Lawrence Tong through the project's GitHub page. There is no self-serve "delete my data" button, no required response time, and no guarantee a request will be granted. A request is considered, not honored, and the operator may decline it in full or in part for any reason, including any reason described in Section 3 (for example, academic honesty review). Submitting a request does not obligate the operator to act on it, confirm receipt of it, or respond to it at all.

See the disclaimer of warranties, limitation of liability, and indemnification provisions in the Terms of Service, which apply fully to this Policy.

7. Students and school context

TAD Chat is built for students of St. Mark's School of Texas and is not designed for use outside that community. It does not ask your age and does not have different handling for younger users beyond what's described on this page — do not post information about yourself or anyone else, including other students, that you wouldn't want stored indefinitely, at the operator's discretion, in a shared, unmoderated database. See also the eligibility, academic-honesty, and conduct rules in the Terms of Service.
